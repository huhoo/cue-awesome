# Start from a recurring job

Choose one task. Begin with one subject and 2–5 public sources. The first session should answer a real question; the second should reuse the saved knowledge.

| Job | Sources | Useful first result | Repeat-use moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Financial disclosure tracking / 财报跟踪 | Two releases from one issuer | Separate GAAP/non-GAAP, reported/constant currency, quarter/year; answer the user's comparison question | Add the next release and inspect newly disclosed scopes |
| Public supplier diligence / 公开供应商尽调 | Official product pages, public reports and announcements | Evidence-backed products, business segments and explicit dependencies; list unknown capacity/certification facts | Add a new announcement without silently deleting old relationships |
| Product and competitor monitoring / 产品与竞品跟踪 | Official release notes, public pricing/specification pages | What features, terms or prices are explicitly disclosed, for which region/date/plan? | Preserve old terms and inspect a new version's qualified claims |

For the first result, provide:

1. Three short answers tied to the user's task, each with source and locator.
2. A readable evidence brief and the reusable package.
3. One clearly scoped next step: supply a new source, correct one claim, or resolve a missing qualifier.

For updates, distinguish new-to-package facts, extra support, incompatible scopes and unresolved same-scope conflicts. No numeric delta proves causality, risk or a business event. Do not infer retirement from absence, an active supplier contract from a name mention, or exhaustive coverage from a short source set.

Use draft definition changes as expert-review suggestions. This public skill does not automatically promote schema, authorize operational actions, or write back to ERP/CRM. Ask which downstream workflow matters only after delivering the source-based result; do not make enterprise discovery a prerequisite to trying the skill.

Prompts / 可直接使用：

- “先用自带样例演示：新材料加入后，哪些事实新增、哪些冲突、依据在哪里？”
- “根据这两份公开报告回答我的三个问题，给我能展开原文的简报，并保存下次可更新的知识包。”
- “把这份新公告加入已有知识包。只列需要我复核的变化，不把没提到的产品当作下线。”
- “Use these public supplier sources to identify evidenced products and dependencies; keep unverified certifications as explicit gaps.”

Adoption evidence is a completed own-source task, a second update, reduced correction effort or a concrete workflow request. Stars/downloads alone do not establish these outcomes. Collect feedback only with consent; do not send documents or contact details to public Issues automatically.
